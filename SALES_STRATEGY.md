## 1. The "WOW" Demo Flow (Your 5-Minute Pitch)
To sell this, you need a demo that feels like magic. Use this flow:
1. **The Capture:** Use the Nexus Editor to show a lead filling out a form.
2. **The Intelligence:** Show the Executive Assistant analyzing that lead in the CRM.
3. **The Action:** Ask Gemini: *"Draft a proposal for this lead and send it via email."*
4. **The Revenue:** Show the lead receiving a Stripe payment link instantly.

## 2. Low-Cost Launch Tactics
- **AppSumo Lifetime Deal (LTD):** Launch a "early adopter" price ($69 for life). This gets you cashflow and users without spending a cent on ads.
- **LinkedIn Authority:** Post one 60-second video of the "AI Researching a Company" feature. This is a massive pain point for salespeople.
- **Niche Verticalization:** Duplicate the landing page and rename the app "The AI CRM for Real Estate Agents." Higher perceived value = higher price.
